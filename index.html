🛠️ Technology

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript only (no backend). The 3D Earth uses globe.gl with real Esri satellite imagery and NASA Blue Marble. Free live APIs: Open-Meteo for weather and air quality, OpenStreetMap for maps and hotels, OSRM for road routes, Wikipedia for places and ExchangeRate-API for currency. Your data stays in localStorage.
